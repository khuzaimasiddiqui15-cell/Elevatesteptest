FAQ

Common Questions

Will people be able to tell I am wearing them?+
No. The insoles sit entirely inside your shoe, completely hidden. Your footwear looks 100% normal from the outside.
What shoe types work best?+
High-top sneakers, boots, and shoes with depth work best. Low-top sneakers work fine with 3cm. For 5cm+ we recommend mid-ankle or high-top shoes.
How much height do I actually get?+
1 layer = 3cm (~1 inch), 2 layers = 5cm (~2 inches), 3 layers = 7cm (~2.75 inches), 4 layers = 9cm (~3.5 inches).
Do I need a bigger shoe size?+
For 3-5cm your current size is fine. For 7-9cm consider going half to one size up, especially in snug shoes.
How do I trim to fit?+
Each insole has printed trim lines on the bottom. Trim with scissors to your size. Fits US 4-9. Takes 30 seconds.
What is your return policy?+
30 days, no questions asked. Email support@elevatestep.com and we will refund you in full.
